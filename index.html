Laundry room rules

  • Press Start only when you are at the machine and it is your turn.
  • Collect your clothes as soon as the timer ends, then press Collected so the next person can use the machine.
  • Washing takes about minutes and drying about minutes.
  • To leave the queue, press Cancel and confirm with your matric no.
Matric and tel numbers are partly hidden on this page. Data is saved in this browser only (see README notes to share it across all devices).
